# iBookshelf — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 8 June 2026_

iBookshelf ("the app", "we", "us") is a personal reading-library app that lets you
catalogue the books you own, track your reading, and sync your library across your
devices. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

## Information we collect

When you create an account and use the app, we store:

- **Account details** — your email address (used for sign-in). Passwords are handled
  by our authentication provider and are never stored by us in readable form. If you
  sign in with Google or Apple, we receive a basic identifier and email from that
  provider.
- **Profile** — a display name you choose, and your avatar and "soul animal"
  preferences.
- **Your library** — the books you add (title, author, ISBN, cover, page count,
  genres, description), and the reading data you create about them: shelves/collections,
  reading status and progress, ratings, reviews, notes, and dates.
- **Cover photos** — if you photograph a book's cover, that image is uploaded to our
  storage so it appears on your other devices.

We do **not** collect location, contacts, advertising identifiers, or analytics/tracking
data, and we do not show ads.

## How your information is used

- To provide the app and its core features.
- To back up your library and sync it across devices you sign in on.

We do **not** sell your personal information or share it for advertising.

## Camera and photos

- The **camera** is used to scan book barcodes. Barcode scans are used to look up book
  details and are not stored as images.
- Your **photo library** is accessed only when you choose a photo to use as a book cover.
  A photo you select is stored with your library so it can sync.

## Service providers

- **Supabase** — hosts our database, authentication, and file storage.
- **Google Books API** — we send search terms and ISBNs to look up book metadata and
  cover images. No account information is sent.
- **Google** and **Apple** — only if you choose to sign in with them.

## Data retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account exists. You can **permanently delete your account
and all associated data at any time** from within the app: **Profile → Delete account**.
This removes your library, shelves, notes, uploaded cover photos, and your account. The
deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

You may also contact us (below) to request deletion.

## Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS and access is restricted so that each user can only read
and modify their own data.

## Children

iBookshelf is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and
we do not knowingly collect their information.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the
"Last updated" date above.
